# Learn Asking the Right Questions as an AI Developer with Phoebe

Six 45-minute sessions on the first meeting of any data project, run by an AI developer: how to get the
outcome, the decision, the current way of working and the real state of the data out of a business
person who knows nothing about data, in words that teach them something while they answer. No code.

**Live site:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-asking-right-questions-as-ai-with-phoebe/

| # | Session |
|---|---|
| 1 | The gap, from the AI developer's chair |
| 2 | The thread end to end, and its data |
| 3 | The AI developer's questions, and the bench |
| 4 | Teaching while you ask |
| 5 | Three projects, and the assistant that only drafts |
| 6 | The mock meeting and the one-page brief |

The bench in session 3 (`assets/asking-live.js` + `assets/asking-bank-ai.js`) runs the same 45-minute
meeting five ways on the same printed fact sheet. On this sheet the fast yes-or-no interview scores
21 and records five beliefs that are wrong; a shuffle averages 30.1; the Six Doors in order score
54; current way first, 55. Every count is computed from the bank file in the browser. The company
and the person are constructed and say so.

Sibling courses share the framework (canonical in the data analyst course), the company and the
engine: data analyst, data scientist, data engineer, AI developer.
