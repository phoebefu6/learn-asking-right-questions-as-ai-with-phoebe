Learn Asking the Right Questions as an AI Developer with Phoebe

An operations director says: "Can AI answer the supplier WhatsApps?" It sounds like a technical request, and the AI developer who treats it as one builds an assistant that says yes to a substitute drug nobody checked. Most of what goes wrong in data work goes wrong in the first meeting, quietly, with both sides sure they agreed. Six sessions on running that meeting as an AI developer: the six doors every discovery conversation has, why current way is the one this role opens widest, the plain second sentence that lets a business person learn how data work thinks while she answers, and a bench that puts one invented operations director with 24 hidden facts in your browser and counts what each way of asking is worth.

Four chairs at the same table 🪑

The same framework, the same company and the same bench engine, built four times for four roles. Each course marks a different door to open widest and carries its own question bank and fact sheet. The framework itself lives in the data analyst course's session 1; this course links to it.

As a data analyst ↗Widest door: decision. Who does what with the number, and by when. As a data scientist ↗Widest door: outcome. What to predict, what a wrong guess costs, how often it happens today. As a data engineer ↗Widest door: data state. Sources, owners, freshness, access, who breaks it. As an AI developer · this courseWidest door: current way.
