## 05-three-projects-and-the-assistant-that-drafts.html · Three projects, and the assistant that only drafts
h1: "Three projects, and the assistant that <span class="accent">only drafts</span>". Mirror DA session 5.
- Outcome in her words: the area managers visit two outlets a day (f02, f21), read from the visit log.
- Three candidates: (1) a decision log: every accepted substitute or price change written to one shared sheet, by the manager, so the record stops living on a phone (the non-ML fix, first); (2) a standard confirmation format written into the supplier contracts that renew in three months (f20) - sometimes the best AI project is a form; (3) an assistant that drafts the plain-OK replies for one manager (Farid, f22) and four suppliers, shows the match it made, and never approves a substitute or a price change. ICE with the rule written down, contested. Google problem framing: optimise the non-ML solution first.
- Pilot: one manager, four suppliers, Priya two hours a week, test = visit log, no patient data in phase one, Q1 budget.
